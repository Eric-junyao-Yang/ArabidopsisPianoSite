Arabidopsis Synthesizer

This project turns arabidopsis transcription patterns into sound!

Here's a quick run-down of what that insane sentence means:

Arabidopsis thaliana is a model organism that plant scientists love to study. It has a relatively small genome and a relatively fast reproduction cycle, but is still a complex flowering plant, which makes it an ideal candidate to research.

Transcription measurements reflect gene activity. Not all your genes are on all the time. Some are only on when needed (like wound repairing genes), or only in certain times during development (like puberty related genes). Others are synced to the day/night cycle (circadian genes).   2. As someone who's interested in samplers and synthesizers, the idea of converting biological data into sound is intriguing.
  3. We know that whole number ratios of frequencies tend to sound pleasent in western music. For example, when you play a sound, then play another sound at twice the frequency, you would interpret that as an octave (same note but higher pitch). Not the most interesting sounding, but certainly harmonious. A 3:2 frequency ratio gives you a perfect Fifth (Do, and So), so on and so forth (see below for more on the music theory side of things). As such, if two genes sound "pleasent" together, it might suggest whole number ratios between their frequencies and might indicate biological relationships.
